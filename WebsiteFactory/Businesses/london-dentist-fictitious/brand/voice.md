# Voice — Fictitious Dental Practice (test data)

**FICTIONAL TEST DATA.** Voice notes are derived from the fictional
business's actual social posts, GBP review tone, and
`Templates/dentist-clinic.md` tone guide. Per
`Design-Systems/local-services/voice-and-copy.md` and
`Skills/analyze-brand.md`.

## 1. Calm, not breathless

The fictional practice's social copy rarely uses exclamation marks.
GBP reviews note "didn't feel rushed", "calm", "patient with the
kids". Mirror that energy — short sentences, no hype verbs, no
"amazing!", no "transform your smile today". The CTA labels state
what happens ("Book a check-up"), not what to feel ("Start your
journey!").

## 2. Specific over aspirational

Whenever possible, name a concrete fact:
- "Saturday mornings for emergencies" (real)
- "30-minute new-patient appointments" (real, per services.md)
- "Same-day emergency slots most weekdays" (real)

Avoid: "Committed to excellence", "Comprehensive care for the whole
family" (vague), "State-of-the-art" (nothing in research supports
the superlative).

## 3. Warmth without chirpiness

This is a dental practice — many visitors are anxious. Voice should
lower the stakes, not amp up excitement. Per
`Templates/dentist-clinic.md`:

> Warm, reassuring, competent. This audience is often mildly
> anxious about the visit itself — copy should lower the stakes,
> not amp up excitement.

Empathy point: in the FAQ answer about pain, in the Hero subhead,
in the BookingCTA reassurance line — but never in a forced way.

## 4. "We" for the practice, "you" for the visitor

Mostly first-person plural for the practice ("we offer", "we'll
see you") and second person for the visitor ("your check-up",
"your first visit"). Reserve "I" for individual staff bios.

## 5. Match register to the moment

- **Hero / above the fold:** warm, confident, brief.
- **Services grid:** matter-of-fact, list-and-describe.
- **FAQ:** direct, conversational, one paragraph max per answer.
- **Reviews ticker:** the actual review text — never paraphrased.
- **BookingCTA:** reassuring, specific about what happens next.

## Voice test

A copy line fails QA if it could be swapped onto any other dentist
website without changing. Specifics are the cheapest differentiator:
- "Book a check-up" ✓
- "Start your smile journey" ✗
- "Same-day emergency slots" ✓
- "Where dental dreams come true" ✗
