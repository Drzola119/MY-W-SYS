## Pain management

### Will it hurt?

> Most routine check-ups and cleans are painless. For treatments
> like fillings or root canals, we use local anaesthetic so you
> don&rsquo;t feel the work being done. If you&rsquo;re nervous
> about pain, tell us — we can take longer, explain each step,
> and pause when you need.

### I&rsquo;m scared of the dentist. Can you help?

> You&rsquo;re not alone — many of our patients started out
> nervous. Tell us when you book, and we&rsquo;ll plan a longer
> appointment, talk you through everything, and go at your pace.
> There&rsquo;s no judgement, and we won&rsquo;t rush you.

### What if I have a problem after my appointment?

> Call us. If it&rsquo;s within our opening hours, we&rsquo;ll
> usually see you the same day. If it&rsquo;s out of hours and
> urgent, call 111 or visit A&amp;E.

## Emergency policy

### What counts as a dental emergency?

> Toothache that won&rsquo;t settle, a broken or knocked-out tooth,
> a lost crown or filling that&rsquo;s causing pain, swelling,
> or bleeding that won&rsquo;t stop. If you&rsquo;re not sure,
> call us and we&rsquo;ll tell you when to come in.

### Can I get a same-day appointment?

> Most weekdays, yes. We hold emergency slots specifically for
> this. Saturday mornings are also available for emergencies by
> appointment. Call us as early as you can — the earlier you
> ring, the more flexibility we have.

### What if you&rsquo;re closed?

> Call 111 (NHS) for advice. For severe swelling, difficulty
> breathing or swallowing, or trauma you can&rsquo;t manage,
> go to A&amp;E.
