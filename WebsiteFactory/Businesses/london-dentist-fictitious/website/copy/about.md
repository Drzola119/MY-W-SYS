**FICTIONAL TEST DATA.** Whatever staff names are used below are
**placeholders** — the actual fictional practice did not publish
identifiable staff on its GBP or socials. See "Staff names" below
for how the Build stage handles this.

## Headline (H1)

> A small family practice in Camden

Per `brand/voice.md`: short, specific, no superlatives. "Family
practice" matches the GBP review feedback and the kids' dentistry
service listed in `research/services.md`.

## Subhead

> We&rsquo;re a small team on Fictitious Street. We take our time,
> explain what we&rsquo;re doing, and treat you like a person, not
> a slot in the diary.

Concrete and traceable. "Small team" — practice size; "explain what
we're doing" — GBP review feedback; "treat you like a person, not a
slot" — GBP review feedback ("didn't feel rushed").

## Practice story (2 short paragraphs)

> We opened on Fictitious Street in Camden because the area needed
> a practice that takes the time to listen. We see patients of all
> ages, from a child&rsquo;s first check-up through to emergency
> same-day care, and we look after a number of families across
> several generations.

> Our work is general dentistry first — check-ups, hygiene, fillings,
> crowns, root canals — with cosmetic treatments available when
> patients ask for them. We don&rsquo;t upsell. If we recommend
> something, we&rsquo;ll explain why.

Per `voice.md` "specific over aspirational": names real services,
real differentiators. "We don't upsell" is direct from
`research/competitors.md` ("no pressure to upsell" — GBP review).

## Staff names (flagged for Build)

**CONTENT GAP.** `research/google-profile.md` documents 5 team photos
on GBP but does not name individual staff in any of the channels
reviewed. The fictional business has no LinkedIn, no team page on
its website (this is a new-website build), and no named bios in the
research corpus.

Per `Standards/quality-standards.md` content integrity rules:
> "Never fabricate credentials, awards, years-in-business, or staff
> names that weren't found in research."

**Build action:** `StaffBios` is rendered as a **placeholder card**
that reads:

> "Team photos and bios coming soon. In the meantime, you can
> [meet us in person](/contact) — bookings are 30 minutes so we
> have time to introduce ourselves."

This is the only honest treatment. We do not invent names, roles,
qualifications, or photos. The placeholder card explicitly invites
the client to populate the section once they confirm names.
