# Competitors — Fictitious Dental Practice (test data)

**FICTIONAL TEST DATA.** Competitor names and notes are invented for
the test. Nothing here refers to a real competitor.

Picked 3 nearby competitors (fictional) for the same sub-vertical
(dentist / dental clinic) in the Camden area. This isn't a full
competitive analysis — just enough context for the Build stage to
position this practice's copy usefully (not by attacking anyone, just
by knowing what other people in the area offer).

## 1. Fictional competitor — "Camden Family Dental" (test data)

- **Position:** Mid-market, longer-tenured, larger team.
- **Strengths (observed):** Strong NHS presence, broad opening hours,
  well-established in the area.
- **Gap this practice could fill:** Warmer tone, more visible
  cosmetic / whitening story, family-friendly framing.

## 2. Fictional competitor — "Camden Cosmetic Studio" (test data)

- **Position:** Boutique, cosmetic-focused, premium-priced.
- **Strengths:** Strong aesthetic brand, good Instagram following.
- **Gap:** They don't really do general dentistry or NHS — this
  practice can be the "everyday + cosmetic" option without
  overclaiming.

## 3. Fictional competitor — "NW1 Dental (Chalk Farm)" (test data)

- **Position:** Newer, modern equipment, mid-to-premium pricing.
- **Strengths:** Modern fit-out, transparent fee guide on the
  website.
- **Gap:** No specific Saturday / emergency emphasis — this practice
  does both, which is a real differentiator.

## What this practice should NOT do

- Don't name competitors on the website.
- Don't compare pricing line-by-line.
- Don't claim to be "the best in Camden" or any similar superlative
  — nothing in research supports that claim.

## What this practice CAN lean on

- "Same-day emergency" and "Saturday hours" — both are real,
  both differentiate from the typical Camden competitor profile.
- Family / kid-friendly framing — backed by the GBP review quote
  about the kids' visit.
- Calm, unhurried visit experience — backed by multiple GBP
  reviews noting "didn't feel rushed".
