# Template — Salon / Spa

## Tone
The most editorial/premium-leaning of the three sub-verticals — this is
where the design system has the most room to take a real aesthetic risk
per `frontend-design` principles, but still grounded in this specific
business's actual brand, not a generic "luxury spa" default.

## Page structure

1. Hero + `AvailabilityStrip` (open now / next available slot)
2. Service menu with price ranges (real, from research — never invented)
3. Before/after gallery — only real client work the business has shared
   publicly; get explicit sourcing confidence before using
4. `ReviewsTicker`
5. Stylist/therapist bios (`StaffBios`)
6. Booking widget / `BookingCTA`
7. FAQ (cancellation policy, what to expect first visit, products used)
8. `ContactLocation`

## CTA priority
Book now > View services & pricing > Call.

## SEO focus
`[city] salon`, `[treatment] [city]` (e.g. "balayage Bristol", "facial
Bristol"), `best [subvertical] near me` where local pack ranking matters.
