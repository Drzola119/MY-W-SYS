# Template — Dentist / Clinic

## Tone
Warm, reassuring, competent. This audience is often mildly anxious about
the visit itself — copy should lower the stakes, not amp up excitement.

## Page structure

1. Hero + `AvailabilityStrip` (open now / accepting new patients)
2. Services grid (cleanings, whitening, implants, emergency — whatever
   research actually found; don't list services they don't offer)
3. "New patient?" block — insurance accepted, what to bring, online forms
   if they have them
4. `ReviewsTicker`
5. Meet the team (`StaffBios`) — real names/photos only if research found
   them; omit rather than invent
6. FAQ (insurance, pain management, emergency policy)
7. `ContactLocation` (map, hours, phone, address — must match Google
   Business Profile exactly)

## CTA priority
Book appointment > Call now > New patient form. Emergency contact should
be reachable from every page if the practice offers it.

## SEO focus
`[city] dentist`, `emergency dentist [city]`, `[procedure] [city]` (e.g.
"teeth whitening Camden"). Pull actual procedures from research — don't
guess a generic list.

## Ethical note
Before/after photos: only use if the business supplied them or they're
public on the business's own channels. Never source dental before/after
imagery from stock libraries and present it as this practice's work.
