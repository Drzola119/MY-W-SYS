# Skill — QA Review

Walk `Standards/quality-standards.md` top to bottom against the built
site. For each item, record pass/fail and evidence (a Lighthouse score,
a specific line/file, a screenshot description) — not just a checkbox.

Pay special attention to the content-integrity rules — these are the
easiest to accidentally violate (an over-eager Build stage inventing a
review, a stat, or a credential to fill a gap) and the most damaging if
they ship.
