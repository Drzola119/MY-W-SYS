# Skill — Analyze Brand

Umbrella check run at the end of Stage 03 (Brand). Given
`research/*.md` and the draft `brand/*.md` files, confirm:

- Colors are derived from an actual source (logo/photos), or the
  fallback is explicitly flagged
- Contrast ratios checked (WCAG AA) for primary text/background pairs
- Typography choice matches the sub-vertical's tone note in
  `Templates/<subvertical>.md`
- Voice notes are specific to this business, not generic
- Nothing in `brand/*.md` contradicts `Standards/quality-standards.md`

Output: either confirm `brand/*.md` as complete, or list specifically
what's missing before Stage 04 can start.
