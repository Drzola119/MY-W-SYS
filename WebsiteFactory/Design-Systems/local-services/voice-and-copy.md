# Voice & Copy — Local Services

- Write from the visitor's side of the screen. They want to know: what do
  you do, where are you, can I get in, why you and not the place next
  door. Lead every page with that, not with a mission statement.
- Active voice, plain verbs. "Book your cleaning" not "Appointments can be
  scheduled." "We fix chipped teeth same-day" not "We offer a
  comprehensive range of restorative solutions."
- Be specific, not aspirational. "12 years serving Camden families" beats
  "Committed to excellence in dental care." If research didn't turn up a
  specific, concrete fact to use, don't paper over the gap with generic
  praise — flag it in `Businesses/<slug>/research/README.md` as a content
  gap for the client to fill.
- A CTA button's label states exactly what happens: "Book a cleaning,"
  "Call now," "Get a free consult" — never a bare "Submit" or "Learn
  more" with no object.
- Match register to sub-vertical: a family dental clinic reads warm and
  reassuring; a boutique fitness studio can be punchier and more direct;
  a med spa sits somewhere calmer and more premium. See the relevant
  `Templates/*.md` for tone notes per sub-vertical.
- Never invent urgency, scarcity, or claims (award-winning, #1 rated,
  etc.) that research didn't verify.
