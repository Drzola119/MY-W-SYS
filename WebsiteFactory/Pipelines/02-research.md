# Stage 02 — Research

**Input:** `Businesses/<slug>/business.md`

**Output:** files under `Businesses/<slug>/research/`

## Steps

1. `google-profile.md` — hours, categories, attributes, real review
   snippets (with star ratings, reviewer initials, dates), photo
   inventory (what's shown, roughly how many, quality).
2. `socials.md` — what's active, posting frequency/recency, tone,
   any brand colors visible.
3. `services.md` — actual services/products offered, and pricing if
   published anywhere. Never infer a service the business doesn't
   actually list.
4. `competitors.md` — 2–3 nearby competitors in the same sub-vertical,
   noted briefly (what they do well, gaps this business could fill).
5. `keywords.md` — realistic local SEO targets based on what this
   business actually does and where it's located.
6. Flag any content gaps plainly at the bottom of the relevant file —
   e.g. "no published pricing found" — rather than filling the gap with
   invented content. `Standards/quality-standards.md` forbids fabrication.

Update `status.md` and `Factory/queue.json` (`stage: "brand"`) when done.
